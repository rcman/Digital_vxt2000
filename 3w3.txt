A 3W3 plug is a type of D-subminiature connector that contains a mixed-contact arrangement of three power contacts and no signal contacts, offering high current handling (often up to 30-40A or more per contact) compared to standard D-sub connectors. Key specifications include a compact, standard D-sub shell size (often DA-size), high-current contacts with gold-plated surfaces, robust construction for industrial use, and options for various termination styles like solder or crimp.  
Key Specifications
Connector Type: D-subminiature (D-Sub) mixed-contact. 
Contact Configuration: "3W3" indicates a shell with three high-power contacts and no signal contacts. 
Current Rating: Significantly higher than standard D-sub connectors, often ranging from 7.5A to 40A per contact, depending on the series and manufacturer. 
Shell Size: Standard D-sub shell size, often a DA-size shell. 
Contact Plating: Typically gold-plated for enhanced conductivity and corrosion resistance. 
Termination Styles: Available in various options, including:
Solder cup 
Crimp 
Through-hole or solder pin 
Materials: Robust construction with steel shells for durability and mechanical stability. 
Mounting: Common mounting styles include through-hole for PCB mounting and panel mounting. 
Applications
Due to their high current capacity and robust design, 3W3 plugs are ideal for: 
Industrial Automation: Powering control systems and machinery.
Telecommunications: Power distribution in networking equipment.
Power Distribution Systems: Providing reliable power in various systems.
Harsh Environments: Applications in transportation and other settings requiring durable connectors.
